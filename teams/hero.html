H.E.R.O.

Help Everyone Respect Oneself is a volunteer group led by Super Seven.

Overview

H.E.R.O. stands for Help Everyone Respect Oneself. Its members organize food drives, help at soup kitchens, look for lost pets, shop for elderly neighbors, and take part in walkathons. Under Super Seven’s leadership, the group does not fight crime.
