Appearance

In the Force Five team portrait, Kid Quickster stands center and wears a pink outfit with a gold chest emblem, wrist cuffs, and boots.

BackStory

Keira Bell was a track athlete who could never understand why the finish line always seemed to arrive before she had reached her stride. Her speed surfaced during a school emergency, when she crossed town several times carrying supplies and messages before the first responders arrived. Force Five gave her a team and taught her how to slow down long enough to make good decisions.

Affiliations

Kid Quickster is Force Five’s youngest member and its fastest messenger. She handles rapid evacuations and carries equipment between separated teammates.

Powers & Abilities

Kid Quickster can run at extraordinary speed, react in fractions of a second, and maintain traction through tight turns. She can carry one passenger for short distances, but heavy loads quickly drain her energy.
