Appearance

In the Force Five team portrait, The Ultimatum stands second from left and wears an orange suit with gold armor and a circular bladed weapon.

BackStory

Saul Mercer spent years as a security contractor, ending conflicts by giving people a clear way to stand down. He left the work after a mission went wrong and civilians were caught in the aftermath. Force Five recruited him for his judgment under pressure and his insistence that every operation include a safe exit.

Affiliations

The Ultimatum is Force Five’s tactical lead in the field. He plans routes, sets boundaries during confrontations, and keeps the team focused on getting people out safely.

Powers & Abilities

His circular weapon is a high-strength alloy disc that returns to his gauntlet after a throw. The gauntlet can release a concussive pulse to disarm opponents or clear debris. The Ultimatum is exceptionally strong and resilient, but his gear needs time to recharge.
