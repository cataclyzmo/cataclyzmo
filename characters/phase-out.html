Appearance

In the Force Five team portrait, Phase-Out stands far right and wears a red suit with a gold chest emblem, belt, and cuffs.

BackStory

Ellis Ward worked in a materials testing lab until an equipment failure trapped him behind a sealed safety barrier. His body passed through the barrier instead of striking it, and he spent months learning where the ability ended and where ordinary matter began. Force Five recruited him when he used that skill to rescue workers from a collapsed transit tunnel.

Affiliations

Phase-Out is Force Five’s specialist in extraction and secure entry. He uses his ability to reach people trapped behind barriers and open paths for the rest of the team.

Powers & Abilities

Phase-Out can pass through solid material for several seconds at a time. While intangible, he cannot breathe, see clearly, or carry another person; he must return to a solid state to interact with the world. Repeated use leaves him exhausted.
