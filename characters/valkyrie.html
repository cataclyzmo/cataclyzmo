Appearance

In the H.E.R.O. team portrait, Valkyrie wears a rose-pink suit with silver trim and an eye mask, and has long brown hair.

BackStory

Freya Anders worked in an emergency room where she learned to stay calm while everyone around her panicked. Her ability to form a protective field appeared during a hospital blackout, shielding patients while staff moved them to safety. She joined H.E.R.O. to bring that same steady care beyond the hospital walls.

Affiliations

Valkyrie is a H.E.R.O. volunteer and medical responder. She supports Super Seven’s community projects and helps coordinate care during large-scale emergencies.

Powers & Abilities

Valkyrie can form a curved energy shield around herself or nearby people. The shield can absorb impacts and deflect small projectiles, but it weakens under sustained force. She is also an accomplished first responder and trained in emergency medicine.
