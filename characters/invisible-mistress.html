Appearance

In the Force Five team portrait, Invisible Mistress stands second from right and wears a purple outfit with a gold chest emblem and matching boots.

BackStory

Nadia Sinclair worked in stage lighting and illusion design, learning how attention could be guided without an audience noticing. Her ability to vanish appeared during a crowded theater evacuation, when she slipped through smoke to lead trapped patrons outside. Force Five recruited her for rescue and reconnaissance work.

Affiliations

Invisible Mistress is a founding member of Force Five. She handles reconnaissance, quiet evacuations, and locating people trapped behind hostile lines.

Powers & Abilities

She can bend visible light around herself to become nearly invisible. With concentration, she can extend the effect to one nearby person or briefly blur a small object. Sudden movement, rain, dust, and strong light can reveal her outline.
