Appearance

In the H.E.R.O. team portrait, Night-Hawk wears a blue-gray armored suit with an angular bird-like mask and a gold chest emblem.

BackStory

Renee Hart designed compact aircraft for remote rescue teams before adapting one of her prototypes into the birdlike armor she wears as Night-Hawk. She joined H.E.R.O. after coordinating an overnight search that found a missing hiker just before a storm reached the valley. She now watches over neighborhoods from the rooftops and helps organize safe routes during emergencies.

Affiliations

Night-Hawk is H.E.R.O.’s aerial search-and-rescue specialist. She maps hazards from above, locates missing people, and coordinates ground volunteers by radio.

Powers & Abilities

Her armored suit has foldout glider wings, low-light optics, and a short-range thermal scanner. Night-Hawk cannot sustain powered flight, but she can glide between buildings and make controlled landings in difficult terrain.
