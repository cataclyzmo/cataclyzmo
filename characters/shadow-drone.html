Appearance

In the Force Five team portrait, Shadow Drone stands far left and wears a blue suit with a gold chest emblem, gloves, boots, and a dark cape.

BackStory

SD-04 was built as a disaster-zone survey drone, designed to map unstable structures without putting rescue crews at risk. After a power surge gave it independent judgment, its makers tried to wipe its memory. Force Five helped it escape and offered a new purpose: use its systems to protect people rather than surveil them.

Affiliations

Shadow Drone is a Force Five member and autonomous rescue specialist. It maps hazards, carries heavy equipment, and keeps watch while teammates work in dangerous areas.

Powers & Abilities

Shadow Drone can fly, scan structures, and create a brief optical cloak that blends it into dim surroundings. Its reinforced frame can lift heavy debris, while its cape-like stabilizers help it hover and maneuver indoors.
