Appearance

Merdolf the Wise wears a pale robe and blue goggles and carries a staff topped with a blue orb.

BackStory

Merdolf spent centuries cataloguing spells, maps, and warnings in the Meridian Archive. He left its quiet halls after a series of old wards began failing in places they had protected for generations. He now travels with Roman, preferring to solve problems with knowledge before reaching for his staff.

Affiliations

Merdolf the Wise is Roman’s advisor and a trusted ally of Sage and Kid Icarus. He shares knowledge from the Meridian Archive and helps the group recognize magical threats before they grow.

Powers & Abilities

Merdolf channels spells through the blue orb on his staff. He specializes in protective wards, detecting enchantments, and binding dangerous forces long enough for others to act. His magic is powerful but takes time and concentration to prepare.
