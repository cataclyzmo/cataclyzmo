Appearance

Kid Icarus wears a green outfit and carries a bow and quiver of arrows.

BackStory

Milo Reyes grew up practicing archery with a bow he inherited from his grandfather. During a storm on a coastal cliff, he discovered he could lift himself into the air and guide his arrows with uncanny precision. Roman helped him learn to use those gifts carefully, and Milo now joins his ally on missions that need a scout in the sky.

Affiliations

Kid Icarus is an ally of Roman and often works with Sage and Merdolf the Wise. He scouts from above and uses his range to keep danger away from civilians.

Powers & Abilities

Kid Icarus can fly for extended periods and make sharp turns in the air. His bow forms luminous arrows that can be set to strike, pin an object in place, or burst into a harmless flash. His aim is excellent, but he is still learning to manage his stamina.
