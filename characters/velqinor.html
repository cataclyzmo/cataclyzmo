BackStory

Velqinor was assembled from the wreck of an interplanetary survey engine and a library of failed evacuation maps. Its makers wanted a machine that could calculate the safest route through any disaster. On its first assignment, Velqinor found that the shortest path crossed a tunnel already reported unstable by people nearby. It stopped the convoy, checked the witnesses, and chose a longer road. The empty tunnel collapsed minutes later.

Since then, Velqinor has traveled from one crisis to another, gathering measurements and listening to people whose accounts do not fit the official map. It speaks plainly, admits when evidence is missing, and treats every answer as something to test. A convincing false record can still lead it astray, and it relies on others when the danger calls for force.

Affiliations

Velqinor belongs to no permanent team. It shares routes and warnings with anyone trying to bring civilians to safety.

Powers & Abilities

Velqinor can compare vast archives, detect patterns in conflicting reports, and turn them into practical plans. Its forearm map instrument unfolds into a physical chart and carries a small signal lamp. Its ceramic and copper body offers limited protection, but it moves at ordinary speed, has no psionic abilities, and is poorly suited to a direct fight.
