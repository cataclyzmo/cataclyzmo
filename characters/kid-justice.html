Appearance

In the H.E.R.O. team portrait, Kid Justice wears a blue suit with a white star on a pink chest emblem, a dark eye mask, and pink pants.

BackStory

Eli Navarro joined H.E.R.O. after organizing classmates to help families displaced by a neighborhood flood. The team’s name appealed to him, but he soon learned that helping people rarely looks like a dramatic showdown. He keeps records, listens to residents, and makes sure promised aid reaches the people who need it.

Affiliations

Kid Justice is a junior member of H.E.R.O. and works closely with Super Seven on community projects. He helps coordinate volunteers and makes sure residents have a voice in decisions that affect them.

Powers & Abilities

Kid Justice has no superhuman abilities. His strengths are careful observation, a strong memory for details, first-aid training, and a talent for calming tense conversations before they escalate.
