Komodo is described as the most cunning member of the Humanoids. In the team portrait, Komodo stands at the far right.

Komodo is one of the five members of Animaliax’s royal family, known as the Humanoids. Read about the team and its world.

Humanoidians combine human-like intelligence with animal strength, speed, and durability. The team’s weaponry includes catapults, crossbows, and primitive bombs.
