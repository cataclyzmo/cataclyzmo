BackStory

Elliot Zaraith, known as Bandit, is a quantum physicist in his mid-forties. He formerly led labs at Notre Dame and is now unemployed. His fixation on time travel and changing what he sees as the wrongs of history alarmed his colleagues. Other professors sometimes found him curled up on a lecture hall floor beneath boards covered in seemingly incoherent notes.

Affiliations

Bandit leads The Society of Rogue. He brought the group together around his plan to reshape history through time travel.

Powers & Abilities

Bandit is trained in quantum physics and wears a super suit acquired by the Society. He carries a high-caliber rifle. His individual power ratings have not been documented.
