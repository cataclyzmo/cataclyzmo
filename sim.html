How the fight is decided

The six base power ratings are added together. A higher total means a better chance of winning, with an upset always possible. Equal totals have equal odds. The browser creates a random seed for each fight so its result can be replayed. Maximum potential and equipment boosts are not included.

Some character dossiers do not have power ratings yet. If either fighter is unrated, the fight uses even odds.
